Our Sanctuary Story

Rooted in peace, crafted with passion

Why We Opened

Piece Of Heaven was born out of a desire to escape the fast-paced noise of life. We wanted to create a lush, green sanctuary in Jaffna where people can slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect.

Our Mission

To serve consistently exquisite, ethically sourced coffees and comforting meals while offering an atmosphere of absolute warmth, mindfulness, and genuine hospitality 24 hours a day.

Our Vision

To become Jaffna’s premier destination for soulful relaxation—a beloved haven where community, nature, and elite café culture seamlessly blend together.
